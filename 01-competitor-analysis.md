# Competitor Analysis — Email Marketing Expert (global English)

Date checked: 2026-10-31
Method: manual web searches for "email marketing expert freelancer", "Klaviyo email marketing expert Shopify freelance consultant" and "email marketing services for small business agency".

**Limits (please read):** I did not have access to Semrush, Ahrefs or similar tools, and I did not crawl each competitor's page. So page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking and backlink data were **not** collected, and I have not invented them. What is below comes from what the search results showed. Open each URL in a browser and use View Source if you need titles/headings.

## What the results pages are made of
For "email marketing expert / freelancer" style searches, the results were mostly:
- **Freelance marketplaces' category pages:** Freelancer.com (many country versions), Upwork, YunoJuno, Malt, Freelancehunt, freelance.nl.
- **Individual profiles on marketplaces:** many Fiverr and Upwork profiles for "Klaviyo Email Marketing Expert".
- **Agencies and directories:** Townley-Jones, Lyfe Marketing, Semrush Agency Partners, DesignRush.
- **Expert directories/blogs:** Mailmodo's "email marketing experts" list, Stripo's blog, DDIY.

Takeaway: for the broad head terms (e.g. "Email Marketing Expert", "Email Marketing Freelancer"), a new personal site competes mostly against large marketplace pages. That is why the plan focuses on brand terms and specific long-tails first.

## Content gaps worth addressing (original content, not copying)
1. **Specific service pages:** competitors' pages are mostly generic or marketplaces. A page per core service (Klaviyo/Shopify flows, deliverability, segmentation) with your real process is a gap.
2. **"How to hire / freelancer vs agency":** marketplaces cover this in a generic way. A practical, honest version can rank for informational + commercial queries.
3. **Deliverability (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** buyers ask for it in job posts; few personal sites explain it clearly.
4. **Transparent pricing/timelines:** your site already shows "From $15" and delivery times. Make sure these are accurate before promoting them.
5. **Real proof:** only publish case studies, numbers and testimonials that are genuine (see technical report).
