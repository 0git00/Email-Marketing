# Getting Found Without Google Search Console

Nothing here guarantees indexing or rankings.

- **Sitemap + robots.txt:** crawlers can find them at the standard URLs. Google also discovers sitemaps through the `Sitemap:` line in robots.txt (already added).
- **Links from public profiles:** LinkedIn, Fiverr, Instagram, GitHub (repo "About" website field), Behance, Medium. Links from sites Google already crawls are the most reliable free way to be discovered.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools** (free, separate from Google): lets you submit the sitemap; Bing data also powers some other engines. IndexNow is also available for Bing-family engines.
- **Crawlable HTML:** your content is plain HTML, which is good.
- **Fresh, original content** (articles/service pages) linked from the homepage.
- **Check status:** search `site:0git00.github.io/Emil-Marketing` in Google. It may take days or weeks for a new site to appear.
- Search Console remains the only official way to request indexing from Google; it is optional, and you chose not to use it.
